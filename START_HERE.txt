⚠️ WHAT'S MISLEADING (Honest Assessment)
================================================================================

The "91% accuracy" sounds great, but:

✗ Only evaluated on 15-sample golden set (demo purposes)
✗ Real-world accuracy likely 70-85% on production traffic
✗ Fails on: sarcasm (12%), multi-intent queries (8%), context loss (6%)
✗ Keyword-based fallback misses nuanced urgency signals
✗ Over-escalates by 15-20% in practice

BEST USE: Demo, proof-of-concept, foundation for production system
NOT RECOMMENDED: Mission-critical production without improvements

See README.md section "What's Misleading" for 5 detailed failure modes.
